## revalidateTag vs updateTag

`revalidateTag(tag, profile)` marks data stale and serves the existing content
while it refreshes. Good for blog posts, product catalogues, docs — anywhere a
few seconds of staleness is invisible.

`updateTag(tag)` expires and refreshes within the same request, giving
read-your-writes semantics. Good for forms and settings, where the user expects
to see their own change immediately.
